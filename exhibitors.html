Exhibitor Information

Show Your Best!

Welcome Exhibitors!

The Cumberland County Exhibition welcomes exhibitors from across the region to showcase their finest livestock, agricultural products, crafts, and more. Whether you're a seasoned exhibitor or participating for the first time, we're here to help make your experience a success.

Livestock Entry Deadline

August 15, 2026

Livestock entries must be received by this date. Late entries may not be accepted.

Exhibition Hall exhibitors do not pre-register — simply bring your exhibits to the hall on Tuesday, Sept 1, 2026 between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM.

Livestock Exhibitors

Our livestock shows are the heart of the Cumberland County Exhibition. We welcome entries in the following categories:

  • Beef Cattle

  • Heavy Horses (haul in/out for competitions - Maritime 4 Horse Classic)
  • Horse Pull

All livestock must have current health certificates and vaccinations as required by provincial regulations. Animals will be inspected upon arrival.

About the Maritime 4 Horse Classic

The Maritime 4 Horse Classic Series is a season-long draft-horse hitch competition circuit held across the Canadian Maritimes. Teams of four draft horses compete in hitch classes driven by wagon or cart. Drivers and teams earn points at each leg of the series, with top finishers qualifying for the year-end Maritime Classic Series Finals. Events are hosted at agricultural shows and fairs throughout the region.

Entry & Contact: Contact the exhibition office for horse pull registration and draft horse entry details.
