3rd Annual Kids Show 'N Shine

Welcome to the Cumberland County Exhibition Kids Show 'N Shine — a kids-only car show for children 9 years and under! Bring an electric powered vehicle your child can operate (or that an adult steers by remote) and show it off to the crowd. No vehicle? No problem — come be a judge and pick your favourite ride. Every child goes home with a treat bag full of goodies!

Event Details

🚗

Bring Your Ride

Open to all children 9 years and under. Bring an electric powered vehicle your child can operate, or that an adult steers via remote, for a kids-only car show!

⚖️

No Vehicle? Be a Judge!

A powered vehicle is not required to take part. Any child 9 & under can come, be a judge, pick their favourite vehicle — and still receive a treat bag!

🎁

Treat Bags for All

Every child who takes part will receive a treat bag full of goodies. It's a fun day for the whole family.

✅

Free — But Pre-Register

This is a free event, but pre-registration is required. All makes and models are accepted. Registration has re-opened — there's no deadline, so sign up right up to show day.

Who Can Participate?

This event is for children 9 years and younger.

If your child does not own a vehicle, they can still participate as a judge and will still receive a treat bag!
