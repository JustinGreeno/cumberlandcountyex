Kids Show 'N Shine

Show off your favourite ride — electric cars & trucks! Drive it and show it to the crowd.

Ages 9 & Under Electric Cars & Trucks No Vehicle? Judge Instead! Register Now →

Register Your Little Car Enthusiast!

Welcome to the CCEx Kids Show 'N Shine! This is a fun event for children to show off their favourite ride-on electric cars or trucks.

Who Can Participate?

This event is for children 9 years and younger.

If your child does not own a vehicle, they can still participate as a judge and will still receive a treat bag!
