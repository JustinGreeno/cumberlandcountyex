Junior Beef Show 2026 — September 3–6

CCEx 2026 · Youth

Junior Beef Show

A weekend built for young cattle exhibitors in Oxford, NS. Showmanship, conformation, sales talk, a farm advertisement contest, our first-ever Cow Costume Parade and more — all working toward the Champion & Reserve Champion Aggregate.

Sept 3–6, 2026 $25 registration T-shirt included All youth welcome Entries Closed Aug 10, 2026

Competitions

Juniors take part across the weekend. To be eligible for the Champion & Reserve Champion Aggregate awards, members must participate in all competitions (the Cow Costume Parade is optional). Full details are in the show package.

🐄 Cow Costume Parade

Sat, Sept 5 · 11:30 AM

Our first-ever cow parade — open to all ages. This class is optional and just for fun!

📷 Photography

Due Thu, Sept 3 · Noon

One photo per member, taken by the junior, representing the farming and show industry in the Maritimes.

Judged by People's Choice.

📝 Farm Advertisement

Due Thu, Sept 3 · Noon

Design an ad for your farm (real or imaginary) on 8½×11 paper — include farm name, owner, address, phone and email. Hand-drawn or computer-generated.

Composition 30% · Originality 30% · Creativity 20% · Professional Appearance 20%.

🎤 Sales Talk

Thu Evening · Barn

Market your animal to the judge (announced day-of — it's up to you to find them). Use photos, registration papers, EPDs, etc. No time limit. Dress in Western attire.

Knowledge 50% · Relaying information 25% · Poise & delivery 25%.

⚖️ Judging

During the Weekend

Test your eye for cattle in the junior judging competition — one of the events counting toward Aggregate.

✂️ Fitting

During Showmanship

Members are judged on the presentation of their animal. Juniors must prepare their own animal for the show ring.

🏅 Showmanship

Sun, Sept 6 · 8:00 AM

Classes run senior, intermediate, junior, then pee wee. Members must wear the supplied t-shirt.

🐮 Conformation

Sun, Sept 6 · After Showmanship

Juniors show one animal. Order: bull calves, prospect steers, heifer calves, yearlings.

💰 Junior Auction

Fri, Sept 4 · Before Horse Pulls

Every participating member brings an item to auction off, raising money for next year's show. You must be present to auction your own item to qualify for Aggregate.

Conformation Classes

Juniors may show one animal only.

Class 1Bull calf — born in the current year (min. 3 months of age on judging day)
Class 2Heifer calf — born in the current year (min. 3 months of age on judging day)
Class 3Yearling heifer — born in the previous year
Class 4Inclusion Class — for individuals of all abilities who need as much or as little support in the show ring

Champion & Reserve Champion Aggregate

  • Members must take part in all competitions to be eligible for the Aggregate awards
  • You must be present to auction your own item at the Junior Auction to qualify
  • The Cow Costume Parade is optional and does not affect eligibility
  • Every junior receives a show t-shirt (required for showmanship) — order your size on the entry form

How to Enter

Fill out the online form above (or the printable entry form), then send your $25 registration fee — cash, cheque, or e-transfer payable to Cumberland Junior Beef Show.

Mail entries & fees to P.O. Box 516, Oxford, NS  B0M 1P0, or email cumberlandexhibition@gmail.com.
