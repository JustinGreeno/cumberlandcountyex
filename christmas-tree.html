Christmas Tree Competition

In loving memory of Keith Moore of ShelBell Farm

A little bit of Christmas in the middle of the fair. Growers of every size, from big lots to small woodlots to backyard rows, are invited to bring their best trees to Oxford and put them up against the county's finest.

$5 per tree No registration deadline Judged Thursday morning On display through Sunday Open to everyone
Register Online Now → Ages 7–21? This counts for Future Farmer → See the Fair Schedule →

At a Glance

Entry Fee

$5 per tree

Enter one tree or several. The fee applies to each tree you bring.

Registration Deadline

None

There's no deadline to register. Sign up whenever you're ready, right up to drop-off.

Drop Off Your Trees

Tuesday or Wednesday

Tuesday, Sept 1 after 5:00 PM or Wednesday, Sept 2 before 8:00 AM.

Judging

Thursday Morning

Trees are judged the morning of Thursday, Sept 3, 2026.

On Display

Through Sunday

Trees stay up for fairgoers to enjoy until Sunday, Sept 6, the last day of the fair.

Where

Exhibition Grounds

Oxford, NS. We'll confirm your drop-off spot when you register.

Who Can Enter

Everyone

Commercial growers, woodlot owners and backyard growers are all welcome.

Youth Ages 7–21

Counts toward Future Farmer

A tree entry is one of the four things required for the Future Farmer Competition, and it earns you points there too.

🎄 In Memory of Keith Moore

The Christmas Tree Competition is held in loving memory of Keith Moore of ShelBell Farm. Keith's love of the land and of a well-grown tree is something a lot of families in Cumberland County remember fondly, and this competition is our way of keeping that going year after year.

Every tree entered is a small tribute, and we'd love to see the barn full of them.

Register Online

Enter the Christmas Tree Competition

Fill out the form below to enter the 2026 Christmas Tree Competition. Tell us your name, the best number to reach you at, and how many trees you're entering. That's it.

Having trouble with the embedded form? Open it in a new tab, or give us a call at (902) 447-3285.

How It Works

Step 1

Register

Complete the entry form above with your name, phone number, email and how many trees you plan to enter (1–10). There's no deadline, so sign up whenever you're ready.

Step 2

Pay Your Entry Fee

Entries are $5 per tree. We'll confirm payment with you after you register. Cash, cheque or e-transfer to the Cumberland County Exhibition.

Step 3

Drop Off Your Trees

Bring your trees to the Exhibition Grounds in Oxford Tuesday, Sept 1 after 5:00 PM or Wednesday, Sept 2 before 8:00 AM.

Step 4

Judging & Display

Judging takes place Thursday morning, Sept 3. Trees stay on display for fairgoers to enjoy until Sunday, Sept 6.

Questions?

The Christmas Tree Competition is chaired by Christine Carter. If you're not sure about anything, like tree size, how many to bring, or when to drop them off, just reach out and we'll walk you through it.
