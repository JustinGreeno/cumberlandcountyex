4-H Youth Programs

The Cumberland County Exhibition is proud to host 4-H competitions and demonstrations on the first two days of CCEx (August 31 & September 1). Watch young farmers showcase their animals and skills in:

Beef Showmanship

See 4-H members present their cattle with pride and skill.

Lifeskills Displays

Interested in 4-H? Visit 4-H Nova Scotia to learn how your child can get involved!

Parent & Guardian Information

For your child's safety, please supervise children at all times, especially around animals and activities. Hand washing stations are available throughout the grounds. Lost children should be reported to the main office immediately.
