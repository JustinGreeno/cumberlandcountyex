Kids Activities

Fun for the Whole Family!

A Day of Adventure Awaits!

The Cumberland County Exhibition is packed with activities designed to delight children of all ages. From meeting friendly farm animals to exciting games and creative activities, there's never a dull moment at CCEx!

Petting Zoo

Get up close and personal with the fluffiest, cuddliest little critters around! Our petting zoo is always a highlight for young visitors — soft, small, and absolutely adorable.

Come meet our fluffy little friends — open daily throughout CCEx!

4-H Youth Programs

The Cumberland County Exhibition is proud to host 4-H competitions and demonstrations throughout CCEx. Watch young farmers showcase their animals and skills in:

Beef Showmanship

See 4-H members present their cattle with pride and skill.

Horse Shows

Western pleasure, equitation, and more competitive events.

Small Animals

Rabbits and other small animals on display.

Interested in 4-H? Visit our information booth to learn how your child can get involved!

Parent & Guardian Information

For your child's safety, please supervise children at all times, especially around animals and rides. Hand washing stations are available throughout the grounds. Lost children should be reported to the main office immediately.
