Frequently Asked Questions

What are the vendor hall hours?

Vendor Hall Hours: September 2nd–5th: 10:00 AM–9:00 PM. September 6th: 10:00 AM–1:00 PM. Vendors must be open during all posted hours.

When can I set up my booth?

Vendors can move in and set up on September 1st or 2nd. All booths must be ready for business by 10:00 AM on September 2nd.

Is electrical power available?

Power outlets are available - indicate your requirement on the application. Food trucks with power & water hookup are $150/week (limited spaces). Standard booths requiring a power outlet should note this on their application.

Can I sell food without a food truck?

Pre-packaged foods and baked goods may be sold from craft booths with proper labeling. All other food sales require a food vendor space and health inspection certificate.

What happens if it rains?

Outdoor vendors should have their own tent/canopy. CCEx operates rain or shine. Indoor craft vendors are protected from weather.
