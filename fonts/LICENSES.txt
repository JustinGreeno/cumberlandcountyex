Notes

The SIL OFL permits use, embedding and redistribution, with two conditions
that matter here: the licence notice must travel with the font files (this
file), and the fonts must not be sold on their own. Neither restricts use on
a website. The OFL also forbids using the Reserved Font Name for a modified
version - these files are unmodified Google Fonts subsets, so that does not
arise.

The Apache 2.0 licence covering Roboto Slab requires the licence notice be
retained; it places no restriction on web use.

Only the latin and latin-ext subsets are included. The cyrillic, greek and
vietnamese subsets Google also serves were omitted as unused. Subsetting is
permitted under both licences.
