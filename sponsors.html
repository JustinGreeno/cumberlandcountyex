Sponsorship Tiers & Perks

Platinum Sponsor - $1,000+
  • Logo on all promotional materials & signage
  • Logo on website with link to your business
  • PA announcements throughout CCEx
  • Social media features & shout-outs
  • Complimentary vendor booth space
  • Event naming rights (e.g. "Sponsored by...")
  • 10 complimentary admission passes
Gold Sponsor - $500+
  • Logo on promotional materials & programs
  • Logo on website
  • PA announcements during CCEx
  • Social media recognition
  • 5 complimentary admission passes
Silver Sponsor
  • Name on promotional materials
  • Name listed on website
  • PA recognition during CCEx
  • 3 complimentary admission passes
Bronze Sponsor
  • Name listed in program
  • Name listed on website
  • 2 complimentary admission passes
Friends of the Exhibition
  • Name listed on website
  • Community recognition at CCEx
